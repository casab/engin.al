[@technoetkinlik](https://instagram.com/technoetkinlik) started as an Instagram page about upcoming techno and electronic music events. Behind it is now one event catalog that feeds everything:

- **technoetkinlik.com:** what's on tonight and this week in İstanbul, İzmir, Ankara and at festivals, with pages for events, artists, venues and genres, in Turkish and English
- **the Instagram pipeline:** posts and stories are generated from the same data and scheduled automatically
- **a submit form** for promoters, spam-checked, landing in a private inbox for review
- media on Cloudflare R2, and editing through a git-based CMS

The site went public on 19 November 2026.
